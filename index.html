❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is basic lighting and audio gear included in the hourly rate?

Yes! Every studio reservation includes standard Profoto/Aputure lighting, C-stands, backdrops, and up to 4 Shure podcast microphones at no extra cost.

How do Jazan University students claim the 25% discount?

Simply check the 'Jazan University Student Discount (25%)' option on the Booking page and present your university ID upon arrival.

Can we hire an on-site sound engineer or lighting assistant?

Absolutely. During checkout on our Booking page, you can add a dedicated Sound Engineer, Lighting Grip, or Multi-Cam Editor to your session.
